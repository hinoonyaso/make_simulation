# Acquisition Plan

Policy: plan by default; never fetch unless the exact source hash, size, redirect hosts, license scope and target path are reviewed. Existing local assets are not downloaded again.

| Family | Method | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| bearing, gears, shaft/coupling, spring, fasteners | authored Blender geometry | Generate CC0 teaching assemblies; mark assumptions; do not claim product or solver fidelity.
| BLDC/PMSM internals, PCB, battery, bracket/beam, fan/heatsink | authored Blender geometry | Generate explanatory separated objects and keyframed preview models.
| KiCad 3D models | official library | Plan only until local library absent/pinned file-level provenance reviewed; bulk clone is excluded.
| NSK/MISUMI manufacturer CAD | vendor portal | Manual/legal review required; no acquisition under current confirmed terms.
| gated Jetson/STM32/Hailo CAD | vendor account/form | Manual required; no bypass.
