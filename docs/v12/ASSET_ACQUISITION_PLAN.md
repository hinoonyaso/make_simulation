## Acquisition gate

Before adding any candidate: record exact part/model and source URL, downloaded archive hash, file formats and part hierarchy, unit/scale check, license text and allowed derivative/publication uses, attribution, and an import/render smoke result. Keep the original archive separate from Blender-converted working files. Prefer a dimensionally accurate simplified author-created proxy when no redistribution-safe CAD source is confirmed. Do not treat product CAD as simulation parameters, and do not alter the PMSM demo trace solely to fit a selected mesh.

## Source notes

- ST's official product page indicates a gated CAD download. An official ST employee response says STEVAL-SPIN3201 eCAD is not published and its Gerbers contain most mechanical information: [ST community response](https://community.st.com/power-management-53/steval-spin3201-cad-files-or-drawing-79647).
- maxon's legal terms limit CAD reuse to product integration/planning and retain notices; this is narrower than general model redistribution rights.
- Search of the repository `assets/` file inventory found existing Pi 5, Livox, Ouster and ZED2i CAD, so those were not proposed again.
