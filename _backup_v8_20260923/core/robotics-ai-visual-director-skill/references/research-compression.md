# Research Compression

For a paper or recent method, keep only:
1. the problem the paper addresses,
2. the one or two ideas that differ from the baseline,
3. the evidence directly supporting the claim,
4. the important limitation or scope.

Do not reproduce a full architecture diagram unless the architecture itself is the lesson. Prefer a simplified baseline -> change -> consequence animation. Label reported values as reported results and keep dataset/task context attached.
