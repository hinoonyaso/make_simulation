# Visual review
Check only visible evidence:
- one immediate attention target;
- readable text/equations at delivery resolution;
- safe margins and no accidental overlap;
- semantic colors remain consistent;
- Blender silhouette, depth, axes, rays and paths are separable;
- no decoration competes with mechanism.
Prefer removal/simplification before adding more graphics.

Identify the deciding feature at delivery size: blocked cell, passage width, contact, force, selected relation. A beautifully framed room fails the lesson if that feature cannot be found. Check semantic overlays against actual lighting; invalid paths and invalid regions need separable line/shape treatment. Prefer a local label or simplified view over a permanent dashboard.


For requested studio polish, compare baseline/candidate at the same source state. Check material separation (metal/rubber/plastic), silhouette and grounded shadows, clipped/washed-out highlights, close-view normal/shading artifacts, and reference/actual path contrast. For camera changes, keep pose and lighting fixed while judging scale, landmark continuity, robot-relative direction and label safe areas. Reject blur/grade that conceals decisive contact or clearance. Check motion-dependent flicker/highlight stability only from moving media and mark it uninspected if unavailable.

Record a specific craft observation, timestamp/source state, owner, smallest supported change and verification target. “Looks more cinematic” without a visible improvement is not a selection criterion. Keep stylistic observations distinct from high/blocker failures and learner understanding evidence.

## Completion evidence for requested refinement

Use the episode's target/criterion/baseline rather than an overall creator-parity score. Examples of observable criteria:

- Reasoning: target selection and current direction appear before the corrective command; a supported changed-condition comparison distinguishes the command from physical response. Expert visual inspection and real novice answers remain separate evidence.
- Typography: the relation stays visible while changed text remains readable through its transition; inspect intermediate frames, not only the final label.
- Studio: the deciding body/contact/clearance remains in frame at cut entry/event/exit; materials and exposure retain their identity across selected views; final edit labels do not cross dark trim, paths or the robot.

For each target, record the baseline defect, owner, criterion, inspected media/range and result. Mark it met only for the checked scope. Avoid universal numeric scores or inventing a threshold after seeing the candidate. Keep a remaining preference as an observation unless it violates the defined target or hides necessary evidence. A newly changed view reopens its bounds/occlusion/overlay checks; unrelated accepted shots retain their review.

## Evidence for deeper narration and studio craft

For a defined narrative target, inspect the final voiced excerpt around the changed discovery link. State what the prior result established, why the next question follows and what visible relation answers it. A viewer who can only repeat the concluding label has not demonstrated transfer; an informed agent interpretation remains different from a first-view novice answer. Do not require a surprise, fixed number of steps, more jargon or a longer runtime. Frame inspection can diagnose an absent link but cannot establish the spoken rhythm of the link.

For a defined surface/view target, compare baseline and candidate at the same solver pose and record the exact benefit: cleaner curved silhouette, preserved mechanical boundary, separable plate/wheel/sensor response, more inspectable contact/gap or stronger path contrast. Identify which factor changed. More saturation or a bigger robot may improve readability while leaving geometry/shading limitations unresolved; report those separately instead of calling the whole scene polished. Keep unchanged successful cameras and lighting eligible.

R09 provides a scoped example: sentence fades and stronger path contrast met their targets, while existing angular STL/goal-ring silhouettes remained observations. The next upgrade may target those explicitly, but cannot retroactively fail the completed R09 goals. A newly requested target requires a baseline, criterion and actual candidate evidence; describe its effect as untested until rendered and inspected.
